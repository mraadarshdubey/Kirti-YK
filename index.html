THE INSIGHT GAP

You May Already Know What’s Happening. So Why Is It Still So Hard To Move Forward?

You are not confused because you are not intelligent. You may simply be caught between what your mind understands and what your emotions still hope for.

What your mind knows

  • This relationship is inconsistent.
  • I deserve better communication.
  • I cannot keep analysing every message.

What you still feel

  • Maybe things will change.
  • What if I am overreacting?
  • What if I walk away too soon?
  • Why do I still feel so attached?

That gap between what you know and what you still feel is where confusion lives. The session helps you slow that loop down and look at the situation from a calmer perspective.
